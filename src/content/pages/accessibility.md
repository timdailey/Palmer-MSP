---
title: Accessibility statement
description: Palmer Motorsports Park's commitment to an accessible website, and how to tell us about a barrier.
---

We want everyone to be able to use this website. It is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## What we do

- Every page works with a keyboard alone and shows a visible focus outline.
- Text meets AA contrast against its background.
- Pages use headings, landmarks and labelled controls so screen readers can navigate them.
- The site works with JavaScript turned off, and respects reduced-motion settings.

## Tell us about a barrier

If something on the site doesn't work for you, email [info@palmermotorsportspark.com](mailto:info@palmermotorsportspark.com) or call (413) 967-3560 (office hours 8 AM–6 PM). We'll get you the information another way and fix the page.
