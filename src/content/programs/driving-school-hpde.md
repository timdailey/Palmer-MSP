High Performance Driving Education (HPDE) is the way onto the track for new and improving drivers. You drive your own car with an instructor in the right seat.

- **Price:** $575 per driver
- **Entries are capped**, so book early.

Before your day, read [Requirements & Car Prep](../requirements/) and the [Cancellation Policy](../cancellation-policy/).
